⚠️ 10 Mistakes That Kill Beginners' Portfolios

```

Traditional LIC endowment policies return only 4-5% IRR — less than inflation! You pay premiums for 20-25 years and get back barely more than you put in. This is insurance misused as investment. Fix: Buy a pure ₹3Cr term plan at ₹10-14K/year and invest the saved premium in ELSS or index funds. The difference in corpus over 20 years is ₹1+ Crore.

If a medical emergency hits and you have no liquid buffer, you'll be forced to redeem equity SIPs at exactly the wrong time — likely during a market fall. A ₹5.5L liquid fund acts as a shock absorber. Rule: Emergency fund first, then SIPs.

Market falls 20-30% and investors panic and stop SIPs. This is the worst thing you can do — you're stopping purchases when everything is on sale! In 2008, 2020 (COVID), and 2022 corrections, investors who stayed invested saw 2-3× returns in the next 3 years. Rule: SIP is auto-pilot — never touch it during a crash. If anything, increase it.

Having 12 different mutual funds does NOT reduce risk — most large-cap funds hold the same Nifty 50 stocks. With ₹41K/month, you need maximum 4-5 funds. Ideal portfolio: 1 ELSS + 1 Nifty50 Index + 1 Nifty Next50 + 1 BAF + 1 Liquid. Clean and simple beats complex every time.

Most people stop at 80C's ₹1.5L limit and ignore the additional ₹50,000 deduction under 80CCD(1B). At 30% slab, this saves ₹15,600 extra per year. Over 20 years, that's ₹3.12L in tax savings alone — and the NPS corpus itself could be ₹36L+ at maturity. It's genuinely free money being left on the table.

EPF withdrawal on job change is one of the most damaging financial habits in India. Example: ₹2L withdrawn at age 27 instead of kept invested = ₹2L × 8.25% × 31 years = ₹22 Lakhs lost! Always transfer your EPF using UAN — never withdraw unless genuine emergency.

A fund that returned 45% last year will likely underperform the next 3 years. Fund performance is cyclical. Small-cap funds top the charts in bull runs and crash hardest in bear markets. Fix: Pick funds based on 5-7 year rolling returns, low expense ratio (<1%), AUM stability, and fund house track record. Stick with them for 10+ years.

If your salary grows 10% annually but your SIP stays flat, your investment-to-income ratio shrinks. The power of Step-Up SIP: ₹20K/month at 10% step-up for 20 years builds ₹3.8 Crore vs flat ₹20K building only ₹2 Crore. Enable "Step-Up SIP" on your MF platform — auto-increase by 10% every year.

FD at 7% with 30% tax = 4.9% effective return. India's inflation is 5-6%. You're barely maintaining purchasing power! FD is for short-term goals and emergency funds ONLY. For any goal >5 years, equity index funds will comfortably beat FD by 4-6% per year, which on a ₹1Cr corpus is ₹4-6L extra every year.
```
