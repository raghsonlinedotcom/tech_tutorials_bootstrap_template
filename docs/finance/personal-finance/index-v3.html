Fewer investments or just starting out
No home loan, no HRA (company quarter/WFH)
Income ≤ ₹12.75L → ZERO TAX!
Simpler filing, no paperwork
Higher take-home cash flexibility
No 80C, no HRA, no home loan deduction
Less incentive to invest for tax savings
💡 Default regime since FY 2023-24. Can switch every year (salaried employees).

⚠️ADVANCE TAX, SELF-ASSESSMENT & PENALTIES
📅 ADVANCE TAX — WHO MUST PAY?
If your total tax liability AFTER TDS > ₹10,000, you must pay Advance Tax in installments.
Due Date% of Advance Tax
15 June15%
15 September45% cumulative
15 December75% cumulative
15 March100% cumulative
Applicable for: freelancers, business income, rental income, capital gains, significant interest income, etc.
📝 SELF-ASSESSMENT TAX (SAT)
Any remaining tax after advance tax and TDS is paid as Self-Assessment Tax before filing ITR.

How to pay: Challan 280 → Income Tax → Self-Assessment Tax → NSDL portal or net banking.

Must include interest u/s 234A and 234B if applicable.
🚨 INTEREST & PENALTY SECTIONS
⛔ SECTION 234A — Late Filing
Interest @ 1% per month on unpaid tax from due date of filing till actual filing date.

Due date: Usually July 31 (extended some years).
Example: Tax due ₹1L, filed 3 months late → ₹3,000 interest penalty.
⛔ SECTION 234B — Advance Tax Shortfall
Interest @ 1% per month if advance tax paid is < 90% of assessed tax.

Calculated from April 1 till date of assessment or payment.
Example: Tax = ₹1L, paid ₹50K advance → Interest on ₹50K × 1% × months.
⛔ SECTION 234C — Deferred Installments
Interest @ 1% per month × 3 months for each missed/short installment deadline.

Applies per installment: June, Sept, Dec, March.
Example: Should have paid ₹15K by June 15, didn't → ₹450 interest.
⛔ SECTION 271 — Penalty for Concealment
50% to 200% of tax amount for hiding income or furnishing inaccurate details. Criminal prosecution possible in serious cases.
⛔ SECTION 234F — Late Filing Fee
₹5,000 flat fee if ITR filed after due date (July 31). Reduced to ₹1,000 if total income ≤ ₹5 lakh. No fee if income is below basic exemption.
📌 SALARY ARREARS — SECTION 89(1)
If you receive salary in arrears (previous year pay given now), you can claim relief u/s 89(1) to avoid paying higher tax due to bunching. File Form 10E before filing ITR to claim this relief.

Submit investment proofs to employer. Buy health insurance, ELSS, PPF contributions NOW.
March 15 — Final Advance Tax Installment
100% advance tax paid. Last chance to make PPF/NPS/ELSS contributions for current FY.
March 31 — LAST DAY of FY!
Last date for all investments to count for this FY. File updated ITR for previous year (AY) if needed.
July 31 — ITR Filing Due Date
File ITR by this date. Pay Self-Assessment Tax. Missing this = 234A interest + 234F penalty!
